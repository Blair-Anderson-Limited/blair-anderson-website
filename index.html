Why Blair ■ Anderson
Client-focused.
Internationally accredited.

Well established in the GCC with a proven track record, we employ high-calibre professionals accredited to internationally recognised institutions. Every project is allocated to a specialist team and fully supported by our directors throughout, to ensure the highest standards of work.

As an RICS Registered Firm, Blair Anderson operates under the RICS Rules of Conduct — the mandatory standards requiring us to act with honesty and integrity, maintain professional competence, deliver a diligent quality of service, and act in the public interest on every engagement.

Our cost management reporting follows the RICS International Construction Measurement Standards (ICMS), so project costs are classified, measured and benchmarked consistently wherever we practise.
